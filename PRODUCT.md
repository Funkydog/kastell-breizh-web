# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary audiences:**
1. **Property owners** in Brittany with character homes (manors, mills, stone houses) seeking professional concierge management
2. **Travelers** seeking authentic, character-rich accommodations in Brittany — typically couples or families looking for unique stays beyond standard hotels

**Context:** Both audiences often browse on mobile while exploring options, researching during leisure moments, or traveling. The decision involves trust, emotional connection to place, and visual appeal.

## Product Purpose

Kastell Breizh Conciergerie connects owners of Breton character properties with travelers seeking authentic experiences. The website must:
- Inspire trust in professional property management
- Evoke the unique charm of Breton heritage homes
- Convert both owners (to sign up) and travelers (to book)
- Communicate expertise, local knowledge, and personalized service

## Brand Personality

**Heritage, Authentic, Refined**

- **Heritage**: Deep respect for Breton history, architecture, and regional character
- **Authentic**: Genuine connections, real local knowledge, transparent service
- **Refined**: Sophisticated without pretension, quality without ostentation

Voice is warm and knowledgeable — like a trusted local friend who happens to be an expert.

## Anti-references

- **Generic hotel booking sites** (Booking.com, Airbnb-style transactional interfaces) — cold, impersonal, no soul
- **Overly modern/minimal** — stark white spaces, brutalist design that ignores warmth
- **Too rustic/countryside cliché** — farm imagery, cliché bucolic aesthetics that feel dated
- **Corporate real estate** — institutional property management with no personality

## Design Principles

1. **Let the properties breathe** — imagery-first layouts where character homes are the heroes, not buried in UI chrome
2. **Warmth without kitsch** — authentic Breton character without falling into rustic clichés; sophistication without coldness
3. **Trust through transparency** — clear information, no hidden complexity, professional presentation
4. **Mobile-native exploration** — designed for people browsing while dreaming of their next trip or reviewing options on the go
5. **Heritage meets modern comfort** — visual language honors tradition while delivering smooth, contemporary UX

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Reduced motion support for animations
- High contrast text on images (no relying on overlays alone)
- Touch-friendly targets for mobile navigation
- French language primary, clear simple vocabulary
