## **III. Technical & Data Forms**

### **Property Identification Sheet**
* **General Data:** Gîte name, type (Manor, Mill, Stone house, etc.), Address, Max people, Surface area, and Number of levels.
* **Specifics:** Remarkable view, isolated, town, enclosed garden, historical charm, pet-friendly, PMR access, and dedicated workspace.
* **Sleeping/Bath:** Bed sizes (200cm, 180cm, etc.), extra beds, number of showers/bathtubs, and provided towels.
* **Kitchen/Comfort:** Starter kit (salt, oil, coffee, etc.), heating type (underfloor, wood stove, etc.), and baby equipment.
* **Tech/Exterior:** Wi-Fi type (Fiber, Starlink, etc.), BBQ type, smoke detectors, and EV charging.
